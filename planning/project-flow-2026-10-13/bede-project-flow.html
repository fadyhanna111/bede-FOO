2 · Murabaha transaction path inside the LMS project

Intended business order from the lifecycle BRD and the supplied Dev API diagram. Each control needs a versioned demonstration and executed test.

01

Customer request

App request and Bede eligibility outcome reach the LMS application.

02

Approve financing

Credit decision approves the financing; it does not yet create a receivable.

03

Purchase & evidence

Wallet journey: asset purchase, ownership, possession, offer and acceptance.

04 · GATE

Record Murabaha sale

Evidence and accepted contract must match; no sale means no disbursement.

05

Disburse

LMS/CBS disbursement follows recorded sale; receivable becomes active.

06

Service & repay

Schedules, collections, accounting, reports and customer statements.

07 · IF EARLY SETTLEMENT

Ibra

Preview → propose → checker approve if required → rebate and repayment.

Ownership-gate challenge

The Dev diagram includes a staff-test sale shortcut that skips wallet stages. Verify its access restrictions and that it cannot bypass required evidence in the accepted environment.

Variant/sequence question

For Phase 1 direct Goods Murabaha, the BRD starts post-approval at Purchase Instructed. An MPO promise is pre-approval in a future variant; clarify the Dev diagram’s post-approval “promise.”
