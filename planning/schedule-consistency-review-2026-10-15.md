## Verdict

This is not a four-calendar-month finish: it extends beyond the earlier illustrative October-January high-level timeline, which was never confirmed as an approved baseline.

## What appears internally consistent

- The root, planning, implementation and delivery start/finish and displayed weekday counts reconcile arithmetically with the screenshot. A 9% overall progress figure is plausible but cannot be verified as delivered scope or acceptance from this schedule alone.
- The chart includes major workstreams from the requirements: configuration, credit scoring, three enhancement groups, Murabaha workflow, integrations, reporting, migration, QA, UAT and production delivery. The saved file has detailed children hidden behind several collapsed rows.
- Performance and Security finish on 26 January, before the displayed production deployment on 27 January. This ordering is sensible, but the required result/evidence and other acceptance gates still need explicit links.
- Workshop 1 is placed on 14-15 October in the saved workshop children, consistent with the recorded 14 October workshop. Future workshop entries are planned dates, not proof that invitations or attendance were confirmed.

## Minimum changes before treating this as a committed delivery baseline

1. Define **technical production deployment**, **cut-over/go-live**, **UAT acceptance** and **production E2E/smoke testing** as separate events. Add explicit finish-to-start links for UAT sign-off, critical-defect closure, migration sign-off, performance/penetration/DR results, and rollback readiness to cut-over.
2. Add the missing migration rehearsals and final data reconciliation, training work, and a 3-6 month hypercare window. Retain milestones for their start/approval dates, but do not represent the work as zero-day tasks.
3. Reconcile Murabaha Workshop 1 decisions and written scope with the BRD/SRS and enhancement subtasks; identify work already done as generic capability, prototype or approved product-specific build. Update acceptance cases and owners accordingly.
4. Confirm calendar and predecessor-link semantics, then re-run critical path and resource checks. Confirm whether the February 16 finish supersedes the earlier four-month illustration. Save and re-open the live QuickPlan file before publishing revised dates.

The screenshot and saved `.qpp` establish plan structure and dates, not actual implementation, test execution, Sharia approval or stakeholder acceptance. This review does not change the live QuickPlan schedule.
