# Bede LMS requirements: source alignment to resolve

Prepared 2 October 2026 from Bede's supplied scope PDF, Bede's 1 October kick-off record/deck and FOO's earlier technical/commercial responses. This is a **document comparison**, not a negotiated scope, compliance determination or accepted contract. The Bede PDF says square-bracketed figures are indicative and subject to agreement (PDF p. 2). References below use actual PDF page numbers.
