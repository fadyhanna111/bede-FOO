Important Notes
• If project timelines are extended beyond the initially agreed schedule, additional budget will be required to cover the extended period. 
This includes costs related to resources, administrative overhead, and potential reallocation expenses.
• If a project stops or blocks due to external factors beyond FOO's control, an evaluation will be conducted to determine the necessary 
timeline adjustment and budget impact. This is mainly due to potential resource reallocation.
• If the SRS phase requires more time and iterations than initially planned, the project timeline will be affected. If no additional changes are 
made to the scope, the timeline will be extended by the same period needed to complete the SRS. Any new changes will result in a re-
assessment to evaluate their impact on the final timeline and budget. Any new changes requested during implementation will also be 
assessed for potential impact on the timeline and budget.
• If the infrastructure is provided by a third party, any delays in delivering any of the agreed environments will impact the timeline. 
Additionally, any issues with the connection between FOO servers and third-party integration endpoints will affect the timeline and 
potentially the budget if FOO is responsible for the connection testing.
• If third-party APIs are not provided on time or do not function as expected, additional budget will be required to address delays in 
integration, troubleshooting, testing, and potential rework. This includes any additional development, review, and testing efforts. 
Timelines will also be impacted based on the duration of the delay and resource reallocation.
• If the UAT phase takes longer and requires more iterations, a re-assessment of resource availability will be done, and the budget will be 
adjusted to cover extended testing efforts, additional iterations, feedback incorporation, and retesting.
• Poor or low-quality in VA & PT (Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing) auditing will result in additional time and budget to re-
check and evaluate any invalid raised issues.
• Any new regulatory compliance requirements not listed in the initial commercial or technical proposal will be subject to timeline and 
budget assessment.
