# Requirements source guide

The [Bede scope and requirements PDF](<Bede - Foo LMS - Scope and Requirements.pdf>) is Bede's requested scope. The [BEDE versus FOO roadmap workbook](BEDE_Requirements_vs_FOO_Roadmap.xlsx) was supplied on 5 October 2026 as a separate working comparison. Preserve both originals; do not treat workbook classifications or recommended actions as Bede/FOO approval.

The workbook has three tabs:

| Tab | Use | Indexed range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BEDE Req vs Roadmap` | Maps 118 Bede requirement rows plus 20 roadmap-only items to roadmap tasks, build location, gaps and proposed actions. | Populated rows 1–154, through column Z. |
| `BEDE Coverage by FRD` | Classifies the 118 Bede requirements against named FRD/BRD documents and the LMS boundary. | Populated rows 1–131, through column J. |
| `Initial Roadmap` | Lists proposed tasks, dependencies, phases and some man-day estimates, including a Mambu option. | Populated rows 1–84, through column O. |

The workbook's cached summary counts list 27 requirements as missing from the roadmap and eight as conflicting; its separate FRD tab lists 50 as not covered and 26 as partly covered. These are the workbook author's classifications, not independently verified product or contractual findings. Several referenced FRD/BRD documents are not among the seven preserved original files, so their underlying coverage cannot be validated from this archive alone. The roadmap is represented in this workbook's `Initial Roadmap` tab, but a separate `Summary Of Tasks` source file was not supplied. An `Initial Roadmap` note mentions leaked HMAC keys; it contains no key value in the inspected cell and is source content, not an instruction to act.

Search the [cell-coordinate index](../search-index/BEDE_Requirements_vs_FOO_Roadmap.json) for wording, then use the original workbook for formulas, layout and filters. The [source manifest](../search-index/source-manifest.json) records its Desktop origin, project path and SHA-256 hash.
